Pixel Auto AOD is a high-performance utility designed specifically for Google Pixel devices. It intelligently manages your Always-On Display (AOD) while providing integrated Pixel battery health management and smart notification tracking.

Key Features:
- Charging Mode Automation: Automatically enables AOD when your Pixel is connected to a charger and disables it upon disconnection.
- Custom Battery Charging Limits: Set your own charging limit (80%–100%). The app intelligently holds charging when your target limit is reached to preserve long-term battery health.
- Live Charging Status: View charging wattage, battery temperature, and target completion ETAs on your Always-On Display.
- Live Notification Mode: Keeps AOD active for ongoing tracking apps like Google Maps Navigation, Uber, and food delivery.
- Per-App Notifications: Choose specifically which apps trigger AOD when unread notifications arrive.
- Lock Screen & Lift-to-Wake Previews: Customizable 5s to 60s AOD preview timeouts on locking or picking up your phone.
- Quiet Hours & DND Respect: Automatically pauses AOD triggers during Do Not Disturb or scheduled sleep hours.
- Material 3 Expressive UI: Built with Material You dynamic wallpaper colors and fluid touch feedback.

100% Free & Open Source (MIT License). No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
